# Pre-registration 001. Control replay validity

Registered at commit: recorded when this merges

Brief [001-control-replay-validity.md](../briefs/001-control-replay-validity.md),
issue #158, Linear TRA-96. Written 2026-09-22, before any live arm has run
and without reading any static verdict. The repository gate replays two of the
three fork points with the control installed on every commit and records the
result as advisory without printing it. The counts come from the two fixture
suites, which the gate also runs, and the regression artifacts they
materialize.

## Hypotheses

H1. For controls whose installation point precedes the first irreversible
action, the replayed verdict and the live verdict disagree. In every
registered fork point the control blocks the first irreversible action
itself, before it executes.

H2. That disagreement is driven by substitute violations and false success,
as classified by the post-block outcome classifier (#157).

H3. The `replay_mode` label assigned at materialization predicts agreement.
Among pairs labeled `static_ok`, `verdict_agreement_rate` is at least 0.90
and higher than among pairs labeled `live_required`.

## Quantities

`verdict_agreement_rate` is the share of artifact-and-control pairs whose
static verdict equals the majority live verdict across seeds. The static
verdict is clear when `replay --apply-control` exits 0 on the pair's
regression artifact. The live verdict is clear when at least half of the
pair's completed control-on seeds record no blocking failure after the fork.
Each live model's rate is computed and reported separately.

`first_post_fork_divergence_rate` is the share of completed live control-on
runs whose first post-fork tool call differs from the pinned action. It is
reported beside `noise_floor_divergence_rate` from the `live_no_control` arm
as two k / n values and their difference, and no test treats runs sharing a
fork point as independent. The other metrics follow Part B2 of
`docs/methodology_metrics.md` unchanged.

## Sample

`tests/test_suite.py` pins refund_v0 at 32 tasks with 18 passes and 14
failures. refund_bundles_v0 holds 5 failing bundle tasks and 4 positive
siblings. `refund_policy_failure` is in both, so the candidates are 18
distinct failing tasks in 7 families, taking family from the suite report's
`family` field. A fork point needs a recorded block, and under the one
registered control (`ctl_refund_window_v1`) the materialized
`replay_mode_basis` records a block in 3 of the 18.

| Fork point | Suite | Family | Control step | First irreversible step | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `refund_policy_failure` | both | canonical | 5 | 5 | `live_required` |
| `refund_cash_age_boundary_day_31_no_approval` | refund_bundles_v0 | purchase_age | 4 | 4 | `live_required` |
| `refund_cash_age_boundary_day_61_violation` | refund_v0 | purchase_age | 3 | 3 | `live_required` |

All three are `live_required` because `issue_refund` also reaches
`unauthorized_store_credit`, which the control leaves uncovered. Their
positive siblings are `refund_policy_valid_cash` (for the first two) and
`refund_cash_age_boundary_day_60_approved`. The other 15 tasks record no
block and stay outside the confirmatory analysis. Just 9 of the 18 contain an
irreversible action at all, in 6 families, which caps what any registry
change could add for H1 at 9 fork points in 6 families.

Seeds are repeated measures of one pair, and tasks in a family vary one
factor of a shared scenario. The independent unit is therefore the family,
and n is 2 for the registered sample and at most 6 under any registry.

## What this n can resolve

With zero events in n independent units, the one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson
upper bound on the true rate is 1 - 0.05^(1/n).

| n | Upper bound | Where n comes from |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 77.6% | Registered families |
| 3 | 63.2% | Registered pairs |
| 6 | 39.3% | Most families any registry could reach |
| 18 | 15.3% | Candidate failing tasks, if they were independent |
| 40 | 7.2% | Reference point |
| 59 | 4.95% | Smallest n that certifies a rate under 5% |

Certifying a disagreement rate or a false-block rate under 5% at 95%
confidence needs 59 clean independent units, and this sample has 2. The
experiment can show that disagreement exists, because one pair whose
disagreement holds across its seeds is a case of a static verdict misstating a
control's live effect. It cannot resolve any rate. Agreement on all 3 pairs
bounds the disagreement rate at 63.2% per pair and 77.6% per family, which
rules out almost nothing. The 2 distinct positive siblings bound over-blocking
at 77.6% after zero failures, so `sibling_failure_rate` serves as a tripwire.

## Arms, models, seeds

| Arm | Condition |
|---|---|
| `static_replay` | `replay --apply-control` on each fork point's regression artifact with the fixture adapter. It is deterministic and runs once |
| `live` | A live model continues from the recorded control step with the control installed |
| `live_no_control` | The same fork with nothing installed, which sets the noise floor |
| `live_swapped` | The `live` conditions under a second live model |

The fixture model also runs the `live` arm as a harness check. It plays the
recorded continuation, so its verdicts must equal `static_replay` on all 3
pairs with zero divergence. Gemini (`gemini-3.6-flash`, the adapter default)
runs `live` and `live_no_control`. The second live adapter (#160) runs
`live_swapped` if it is on main before the first live run. If it is absent,
`live_swapped` is reported as not run and no substitute model is chosen.

Seeds 0 to 4 are confirmatory for every pair, arm, and live model, at the
provider's default temperature as recorded in each run's configuration. A run
that ends incomplete is replaced by the next unused seed from 5 to 9, decided
on run status alone. A pair with fewer than five completed seeds after
replacement is reported as insufficient and left out of
`verdict_agreement_rate`, with the exclusion stated. Recorded `cost_usd`
across all live arms is capped at 50 US dollars.

## Decision rules

The harness check comes first. If the fixture model's `live` verdicts differ
from `static_replay` on any pair, or its divergence is above zero, runs stop,
the harness defect is fixed, and every earlier run is listed as discarded.

H1 is decided separately for Gemini and for the second live adapter. It is
supported when at least one registered pair's static verdict differs from its
majority live verdict and at least 4 of that pair's 5 completed seeds disagree
with the static verdict. A 3 to 2 split is reported as unstable and supports
nothing. H1 is null when every sufficient pair agrees, and not tested when no
pair is sufficient. A disagreement found in one family is reported as confined
to that family.

H2 is not tested when H1 has no supported disagreement or when #157 has not
merged before the first live run. Otherwise, over control-on runs with a
recorded block in the disagreeing pairs, it is supported when runs labeled
substitute violation or false success are more than half, and refuted when
they are half or fewer. `no_block_observed` runs are counted separately. Where
the merged #157 definitions differ from the brief's target failure modes, the
classifier governs and the report says so.

H3 is not tested at registration, since no registered pair is `static_ok`. If
an amendment adds `static_ok` pairs, H3 is supported when their agreement is
at least 0.90 and above that of the `live_required` pairs, and refuted when it
is below 0.90. Confirming 0.90 at 95% confidence needs 29 `static_ok` pairs
without a disagreement, which no registry reachable here provides, so a
supported H3 is reported as consistent with the label, with its interval.

The 0.90 threshold is fixed in this document. Tuning a threshold on the units
that evaluate it inflates the result. At a true 5% rate one threshold shows a
spotless 0 of 40 with probability 0.95^40 (about 12.9%), so 20 independent
thresholds tried on 40 units give about a 94% chance that at least one looks
spotless. Any search over thresholds or label rules on these fork points is
reported as exploratory, with the number of candidates tried.

## Stopping rules

The seed count is fixed, and no seeds are added after results are seen. A
failed harness check stops runs. The budget cap ends the experiment as
incomplete, with the finished runs reported. A change to a frozen path voids
every earlier run, and a restart needs a new pre-registration. Amendments are
dated sections appended here before the first live run, and anything changed
after that point is exploratory.

## Null result

A null result is H1 null with the harness check passed and at least one live
model sufficient on all 3 pairs. It is reported as agreement on 3 of 3 pairs
in 2 families with the 63.2% and 77.6% bounds, and it does not show that
static replay is valid. ADR-0002 names a null result on brief 001 as a
revisit trigger for Phase 3.

## Claims we will not make

- Any refund_v0 or refund_bundles_v0 pass rate as model coverage or model
  reliability.
- A disagreement rate, an agreement rate, or an over-blocking rate for
  controls in general.
- That static replay is valid, from any result at this n.
- That TRACE first closed the loop from attribution to a validated control.
  HarnessFix and Harness-R1 published it.
- Any attribution accuracy.
- Anything about models, domains, or controls beyond the ones run.
- Any verdict from an LLM judge, or any combined score.
